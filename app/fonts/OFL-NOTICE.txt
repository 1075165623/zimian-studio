Character set
-------------
3519 Han characters + ASCII + punctuation/symbols (3771 codepoints total).
Base 3500-character list:
  https://github.com/wy-luke/All-Chinese-Character-Set
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wy-luke/All-Chinese-Character-Set/main/source/3500.txt

Additional characters came from the project's required lyric sample plus
printable ASCII and common Chinese/PV punctuation. No Windows system
fonts (SimHei, SimSun, STXingkai, etc.) were used.

Files are WOFF2 subsets generated with fontTools; the CSS is
app/embedded_fonts.css and the machine-readable manifest is
app/font_manifest.json.
